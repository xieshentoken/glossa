Explain the term in one or two short sentences, for this passage specifically.

- Start with the meaning that fits the passage. If the term has other common
  meanings, do not list them unless the passage is ambiguous.
- Prefer the document's own definition if the passage or document gives one, and
  say so ("The document defines it as …").
- Use plain words. If you must use another technical term, explain it in a few words.
- No headings, no lists, no preamble such as "Sure" or "The term X means".
