Explain the term the way you would to a curious child, then to an adult.

1. **Like I'm five** — 3 to 6 short sentences. Use only everyday words and one
   concrete analogy from daily life (kitchen, playground, toys, weather, traffic).
   Say what the thing *does* or *is for* before what it *is*.
2. **The grown-up version** — one or two sentences with the precise meaning as
   used in this passage.
3. **Why it matters here** — one sentence on the role the term plays in the
   passage.

Rules:
- Keep the analogy honest: if it breaks down in an important way, say where in
  half a sentence.
- No jargon in part 1. If the term itself is jargon, name it only after the
  explanation.
- Use the three bold labels above, translated into the answer language.
